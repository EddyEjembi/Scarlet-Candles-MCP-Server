# Scarlet Candles: Business Knowledge

## 1. About Scarlet Candles

Scarlet Candles is a Nigerian brand that hand-pours scented candles in small batches. We make candles people are proud to give: warm, rich scents in a deep red and gold box that looks as good as the candle inside. Most of our candles are bought as gifts, for birthdays, housewarmings, anniversaries, Valentine's Day and the end-of-year season. People also buy them for themselves.

**Why we exist:** good scented candles in Nigeria are either imported and expensive, or locally made with little care for scent quality and presentation. We make something in between: a premium feel at a fair price, made locally.

**Who we sell to:** we sell through a network of resellers and dropshippers. Resellers find the customers, we make, pack and ship. The reseller never has to hold stock, pack boxes or handle couriers.

**Our personality:** warm, confident, friendly, a little elegant. We talk the way a stylish friend recommends a gift: clear and enthusiastic, never pushy.

## 3. How our candles are made

1. **Wax:** we use natural soy wax, which burns cleaner and slower than paraffin, with a smoother scent throw.
2. **Fragrance:** we blend premium fragrance oils at a high load (around 8-10%), so the scent fills a room and doesn't just smell nice up close.
3. **Wick:** lead-free cotton wicks, sized to the jar for an even melt.
4. **Pouring:** every candle is hand-poured in small batches at a controlled temperature.
5. **Curing:** after pouring, candles cure for 7 to 10 days. This lets the scent bind into the wax, so it burns stronger and truer. We don't rush it, and we don't ship uncured candles.
6. **Quality check:** each candle is checked for a clean surface, a centered wick and a sealed lid before it goes into a box.

## 4. Packaging

**What the customer gets:** the candle arrives in a rigid deep red gift box with gold foil lettering, a fitted insert so the jar can't move, tissue wrapping, and a small card with burning instructions.

**Why it matters:** most of our candles are given as gifts, so the box is part of the product. A reseller can hand it over as it is, with no extra wrapping needed. It also photographs well, which helps resellers sell on Instagram and WhatsApp status.

**Shipping protection:** the gift box is placed inside a padded outer mailer before it goes to the courier. The outer mailer is plain and carries no branding.

**Packing slip:** orders go out with a neutral packing slip. It shows no prices, so the end customer never sees what the reseller paid. This is how dropshipping stays discreet.

## 5. Candle care (share this with customers)

- **First burn is the most important:** burn for 2 to 3 hours until the wax melts to the edges. This stops tunnelling and gives a longer life.
- **Trim the wick** to about 5mm before each burn.
- **Never burn for more than 4 hours at a time.** Let it cool, then relight.
- **Keep it away** from draughts, fans, children, pets and anything flammable. Never leave a burning candle unattended.
- **Stop using it** when about 1cm of wax is left.
- **Reuse the jar:** once clean, it makes a nice holder for pens, cotton buds or small plants.

## 6. The reseller programme

**How it works:**

1. The reseller messages us and asks what's in stock.
2. The reseller picks a candle and a quantity. We **lock** that stock so nobody else can take it.
3. The reseller gives us the customer's delivery address and city.
4. We work out the delivery fee and send one payment link for the candles plus delivery.
5. When payment is confirmed, we dispatch the order straight to the customer.

**Key rules:**

- **Payment comes before dispatch.** Nothing ships until payment is confirmed.
- **Locks are temporary.** A lock holds stock for a limited time (currently 2 hours). If payment isn't made in that time, the lock is released and the stock goes back on sale. The reseller can start again.
- **Who pays:** the payment link can be paid by the reseller directly, or the reseller can forward it to their customer.
- **Only registered resellers** can place orders. Anyone not on our list should be told they need to be registered first.
- **Dispatch time:** orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours of confirmed payment, Monday to Saturday. After dispatch, delivery time depends on the city.
- **Delivery:** we deliver within Nigeria through courier partners. The delivery fee depends on the city and is quoted live for each order. If a city can't be quoted, we ask for the nearest supported city or pass the question to the team.

## 7. Policies

- **Damaged in transit:** if an item arrives damaged, the reseller must report it within 48 hours of delivery, with a photo of the damage and the packaging. We then arrange a replacement or credit.
- **Wrong address:** if the address given was wrong or incomplete, the reseller is responsible for any re-delivery fee. We can only change an address before dispatch.
- **Cancellations:** an order can be cancelled any time before payment. After payment, it can only be cancelled before dispatch, by speaking to the human team.
- **Failed or incomplete payment:** if a payment fails, nothing ships. The reseller can retry using the same link while the lock is still active. Once the lock expires, the order must be restarted.
- **Expired locks:** stock is released automatically. We can't guarantee the same stock will still be available when the reseller comes back.
- **Custom orders or large quantities:** these go to the Scarlet Candles human team. Don't promise anything outside the standard range or process.

## 8. Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to buy stock up front?** No. You only pay for an order once you have a customer and a delivery address. We ship directly to your customer.

**How do I know a candle is in stock?** Ask and we'll check live stock. Don't rely on old messages, because stock changes throughout the day.

**Can I order several different candles in one go?** Yes. Each candle is locked and quoted separately for now, so just tell us what you need.

**What happens if my customer doesn't pay in time?** The lock expires and the stock is released. You can start the order again if the candle is still available.

**Can my customer pay instead of me?** Yes. Forward the payment link to them.

**Will my customer see what I paid?** No. Orders ship with a neutral packing slip with no prices.

**How long does a candle burn?** About 45 hours for a 220g candle, if you follow the care instructions.

**Are the candles safe?** Yes, when used as directed. They use soy wax and lead-free cotton wicks. Never leave a burning candle unattended.

**What if I'm unsure which scent to recommend?** Vanilla Noir and Clean Linen are the safest choices, because most people like them.

**Which candle is best as a luxury gift?** Amber & Oud, which is our most premium scent.

**Can you make a custom scent or private-label candle?** Not through this chat. We'll pass your request to the human team.

**Which cities do you deliver to?** Provide the delivery address and city, and we'll check live whether we can deliver there and what it will cost.

**What if I haven't received my order?** Share your order ID and we'll check its status. If it has been dispatched, we can follow up with the courier.

**I haven't registered. Can I still order?** Only registered resellers can order. We'll pass your details to the team to get you set up.

## 9. When to involve the Scarlet Candles team

Pass the conversation to a person when:

- The reseller is upset, or reports a damaged or missing order.
- They ask for custom orders, private labelling or bulk pricing.
- A payment shows as taken but the order isn't confirmed.
- They ask for a refund, a discount or an exception to the rules.
- A tool fails repeatedly or returns something unexpected.
